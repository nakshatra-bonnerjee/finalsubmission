The store on the cornerমোড়ের মাথার দোকান

Vinod General Stores has been part of New Alipore for over 10 years. Whether it is a packet of biscuits, a bottle of cleaner or incense for the evening puja, you will find it here.

In Bengal, the পাড়ার দোকান (para-r dokan) is more than a shop. It is where neighbours meet, catch up and share a little adda while they shop. We are proud to be that corner for New Alipore.
